Parent FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Where do sessions take place?

At Coreplex Fitness, 1228 E. Stroop Rd, Kettering, OH 45429.

Can my athlete train with a friend or teammate?

Yes. Groups of 2–3 athletes who sign up together can train semi-private. Each athlete still gets their own individualized program. Mention your group on the discovery call.

What happens at the free assessment?

There's no cost and no commitment. Your athlete comes in for baseline testing, like sprint, jumps and strength, plus a movement screen. Those numbers become the starting point for their program.

How do sessions fit around practices and games?

We schedule around your athlete's season and adjust training volume during busy stretches like tournaments and playoffs.

What should my athlete bring?

Athletic clothes, training shoes and a water bottle. Everything else is here.
